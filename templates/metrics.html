Pipeline Preprocessing Steps (Documented in Code)

1. Aspect Normalization: Downscales oversized camera resolutions (>1920px) preserving aspect ratio and converting to contiguous memory layout.
2. Target Class Filter: Strictly limits outputs to Elephant, Tiger, and Monkey classes, discarding extraneous COCO labels.
3. Worker Suppression Stage: Runs simultaneous human detection. If an animal box intersects >20% with a human worker, it is suppressed to prevent false alarms.
